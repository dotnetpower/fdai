    How observation affinity works

    Express a preference

    Match a resource group or specific resources. Strong preference gives the target more consideration for available extra observation capacity, not exclusive monitoring.

    Respect existing limits

    Baseline checks, serious incidents, access permissions and budget limits take precedence. Affinity cannot grant access, increase the budget or authorize an action.

    Verify the application

    A saved preference is not proof of extra observation. In this proposal, overlapping rules use the strongest matching preference without stacking. Missing evidence remains unknown.
