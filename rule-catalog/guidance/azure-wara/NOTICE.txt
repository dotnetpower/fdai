FDAI additions and limitations

The mechanism explanations, per-recommendation observation bindings and typed
conceptual diagrams are repository-authored additions. No private presentation,
customer screenshot or third-party diagram is copied. The diagrams are not
Microsoft-authored figures or discovered deployment topologies.

The source metadata is retained with English fallback and ASCII punctuation
normalization. Shared service and mechanism context is reused across related
recommendations; it is not 456 independent technical or live-environment
validations. The generated coverage report records the three individually
authored pilots, sixteen expanded conditional guides, their scoped public
documentation review and the remaining review limits.

This package is advisory reference content, not a finding, an approval or a
grant of operational execution rights.
